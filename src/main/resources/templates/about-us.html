Our Story

We're rebuilding how Bangladesh
discovers and rents space.

RentHub BD was born out of a simple frustration — finding a decent place to rent in Dhaka shouldn't require scrolling through hundreds of Facebook posts, chasing unreliable brokers, or driving around looking for To-Let signs. We built the platform we wished existed.

◎

Our Vision

To become the default way Bangladesh rents.

A country where every renter — whether a student in Mirpur or a multinational opening a Gulshan office — finds their next space through a single trusted platform. Where property listings are structured, verified and priced honestly. Where the friction, waste and middlemen of today's rental market are simply gone.

⚑

Our Mission

Make every rentable space discoverable, comparable and verifiable.

We do this by giving owners a structured way to publish accurate listings, giving renters powerful filters to find exactly what fits, and giving administrators the tools to keep the marketplace clean. No noise. No hidden fees. No wasted site visits. Just a marketplace that respects everyone's time.

What we stand for

Four principles, every decision

These aren't posters on a wall — they're the filters every feature goes through before it ships.

01

Transparency

Rent plus service charge, parking and utilities shown as one total. What you see is what you'll actually pay. No asterisks, no surprises at signing.

02

Verification

Every listing passes through human review before going live. Suspicious accounts get deactivated. Reports get investigated. Quality is a feature, not a suggestion.

03

Simplicity

Search by what actually matters — location, size, budget, facilities. Not fifteen dropdowns designed to hide the real options.

04

Local first

Built in Dhaka for Bangladesh. Every category, filter and city name reflects how renting actually works here — from bachelor messes to Banani showrooms.

The path ahead

Where we're going

Listings across residential, commercial, industrial and other spaces. Verified owners. Structured search. Direct messaging. Admin moderation. This is what's live today.

Next

Ahead

Chattogram, Sylhet, Khulna, Rajshahi. Every major city where the same problems — fragmented listings, unreliable middlemen — deserve the same clean solution.

Our promise

To renters and owners alike

We will never sell your data. We will never let a pay-to-win listing outrank a better one. We will never grow so fast that quality slips. Every feature we build will pass a simple test — does it save renters time or owners money? If the answer is no, it doesn't ship.
